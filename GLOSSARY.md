# Deadpixel

Deadpixel is a local-first raster editor.

## Language

### Signal Rack

An ordered chain of nondestructive effects applied to the document output or to a layer.

### Painted Signal Rack mask

Editable alpha coverage that controls where a Signal Rack effect applies. Effects that share a mask see the same paint changes, and **DUP** gives the selected effect an independent copy.
